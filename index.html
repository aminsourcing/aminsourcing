Our Services

Complete Sourcing Support, From Inquiry to Delivery

Product Sourcing & Supplier Verification

We find reliable factories for your exact product and spec, verify business licenses and production capacity, and shortlist only qualified suppliers.

Price Negotiation & Order Management

We negotiate the best price and payment terms for you, place orders, and manage production schedules so your delivery stays on time.

Quality Control & Factory Audits

Pre-shipment inspection, in-line QC and random testing on every order. We check your goods before they leave the factory — and report back to you.

Shipping & Logistics Coordination

Sea, air and land freight, customs clearance, and delivery planning to Iraq, Syria, Morocco and worldwide — including transit shipping routes.
